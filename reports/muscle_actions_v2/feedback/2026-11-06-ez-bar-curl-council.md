# Findings: council review of EZ-Bar Curl (revision c16d4acbd029), 2026-11-06

The council (owner + Claude Opus 5.5 reviewer) rejected the re-traced EZ-Bar
Curl. Movement roles were judged correct; stabilization was not. Apply these
findings to the group being revised.

## Vocabulary changes (now in the vocabulary above)

- New demand `grip`: sustained finger and thumb flexion holding a bar, handle,
  dumbbell or body weight. Finger flexors (flexor digitorum superficialis and
  profundus), flexor pollicis longus and the hand intrinsics serve it.
- New demand `wrist_stability`: holding the wrist neutral against a load in the
  hand. Wrist flexors and extensors serve it through co-contraction.
- New demand `shoulder_position_hold`: holding the upper arm still while the
  load acts further down the limb (upper arms beside the torso in a curl;
  overhead in a triceps extension). Isometric deltoid and shoulder
  flexor/extensor work. The anterior deltoid is the main resister of the
  shoulder-extension moment in a curl.
- `postural_stabilization` now explicitly includes the deep trunk stabilizers
  (multifidus, transversus abdominis, internal oblique), not only trunk
  extensors.
- `intra_abdominal_pressure` is deliberate bracing under heavy load:
  transversus abdominis, obliques, rectus abdominis, diaphragm and pelvic floor.

## Specific errors found

1. The long head of triceps brachii was given `glenohumeral_stabilization`.
   In a curl it is an antagonist (shoulder and elbow extensor), not a
   humeral-head stabilizer. Remove that role unless it is genuinely
   justified, and say why in the rationale.
2. The anterior (clavicular) deltoid had no stabilization role for holding
   the arm in position. Give it `shoulder_position_hold`; also consider the
   other muscles that resist shoulder flexion or extension isometrically.
3. Transversus abdominis had no `postural_stabilization` role, so a standing
   exercise left the deep core at zero. Give the abdominal wall muscles the
   roles the revised definitions above call for.
4. Finger and wrist flexors had no way to be engaged when a loaded bar is
   held. Give forearm and hand muscles `grip` and `wrist_stability` weights.

Keep correct entries unchanged; the movement actions were judged correct.
