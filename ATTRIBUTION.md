# Third-party content

## Anatomy models

The musculoskeletal meshes in `build/anatomy/regions/` are extracted and adapted
from the Z-Anatomy atlas, which incorporates BodyParts3D:

- **Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy - CC-BY-SA 4.0**
  (<https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy>)
- **BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan**

BodySim's changes: extraction of the 960 musculoskeletal structures, material
replacement, metadata, and regional GLB export (see `docs/anatomy-licensing.md`
and `data/anatomy/sources/z_anatomy.json`). These adapted meshes are
distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0
(<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>).

## Exercise catalog and photos

(<https://github.com/yuhonas/free-exercise-db>), released under the Unlicense.
